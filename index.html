Services

What we do

Design

From SoC selection to a reviewed layout the factory can build and test.

  • Architecture and SoC / module selection
  • Schematic capture and multilayer PCB layout
  • High-speed interface routing
  • Power, regulation and thermal design
  • Design for manufacture and test
  • Prototype builds through our partner: PCB fabrication, stencil, SMT and assembly
  • Mechanical, enclosure, cable and connector integration
  • Reference-design adaptation and cost-down redesign
  • Feasibility studies and technical due diligence
  • Design review of existing boards

Engineering

Your board running maintained, mainline-based Linux, from bootloader to drivers.

  • Board bring-up and hardware validation
  • Bootloader, kernel and BSP
  • Device trees, drivers and peripheral enablement
  • On-device AI integration
  • OS images, provisioning and field-update design
  • Production test fixtures and software
  • Security review of vendor boot firmware and of our own firmware changes
  • Upstream-first, long-term maintenance

Supply chain

Parts sourced and costed to a delivered price, builds inspected and certified, finished goods shipped.

  • Authorised-distribution and open-market quotes compared; suppliers qualified
  • BOM costed to a delivered, duty-paid price; tooling, enclosure and packaging vendors
  • Alternates qualified; shortage, allocation and end-of-life cover
  • Pilot builds, yield analysis and quality plans
  • Through our partner: factory audits, first-article and pre-shipment inspection, counterfeit screening and incoming inspection
  • EMC pre-scan; FCC, CE, UKCA and equivalent certification
  • RoHS, REACH and materials declarations; labelling, packaging and marketplace listing requirements
  • Through our fulfillment partner: consolidation, kitting, labelling and repack
  • Export documentation and HTS classification; air and ocean freight, DDP or FOB; customs and duty review

Process

How we work

We scope to the actual gap rather than to a fixed package, and we say plainly when a project should not be built.

Ways to engage

  • Advisory review — a second opinion on an architecture, schematic or BOM
  • Fixed-scope design — a design project with a defined deliverable
  • Sourcing engagement — sourcing on its own, without a design project
  • Turnkey build to order — from concept through certified, delivered units
  1. Scope Requirements, constraints, volumes, target cost, and the regulations the product must meet.
  2. Architect Silicon and module selection, block diagram, preliminary BOM, and a costed set of options rather than a single answer.
  3. Design Schematic, multilayer PCB layout, power and thermal design.
  4. Prove Bring-up on real hardware from the first day, security review of vendor boot firmware and of our own firmware changes, and EMC pre-scan.
  5. Industrialize Design for manufacture and test, test fixtures, pilot run, yield and quality plans, and certification.
  6. Deliver Consolidation, kitting and labelling through our fulfillment partner, export documentation, and air or ocean freight, DDP or FOB.
  7. Sustain Software maintenance on mainline-based Linux, software updates, and shortage and end-of-life cover over the product's life.
