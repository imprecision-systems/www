Services

One team from idea to shipped units

Most product failures happen at the seams — between the schematic and the factory, between the driver and the SoC vendor, between the BOM and what is actually purchasable this quarter. We work across all of them.

01

Electronics consulting

Independent technical judgement before you commit capital.

  • Architecture and SoC / module selection
  • Feasibility studies and technical due diligence
  • BOM and landed-cost modelling
  • Vendor and contract-manufacturer evaluation
  • Roadmap, lifecycle, and second-source planning
02

Hardware design

Schematic through fabrication-ready release.

  • Schematic capture and multilayer PCB layout
  • DDR, PCIe, USB, MIPI and other high-speed routing
  • Power tree, regulation, and thermal design
  • Mechanical, enclosure, and connector integration
  • Signal-integrity and design review of existing boards
03

Embedded engineering

Silicon is only as good as the software that brings it up.

  • Board bring-up and hardware validation
  • Bootloader, kernel, and BSP work
  • Device trees, drivers, and peripheral enablement
  • OS images, provisioning, and field update paths
  • Open-source stacks and upstream-first maintenance
04

Manufacturing & NPI

The transition from working prototype to repeatable product.

  • Design for manufacture and design for test
  • Factory test fixtures and production test software
  • Pilot builds, yield analysis, and process tuning
  • Quality plans, inspection criteria, and RMA loops

Supply chain

Parts that exist, at a price that holds, when you need them.

Compliance & release

A design is only finished when the factory can build it twice.

We measure our work by what comes off the line in volume, not by what passed on the bench once. That is why design, embedded software, manufacturing, and sourcing sit in the same team here rather than in four contracts that blame each other.

How we work

Engaged at whatever depth the problem needs

Some clients need a second opinion on a schematic. Others hand us a concept and take delivery of palletised, certified product. We scope to the actual gap rather than to a fixed package, and we say plainly when a project should not be built.

  • Advisory retainer
  • Fixed-scope design project
  • Embedded engineering block
  • NPI & factory transfer
  • Turnkey build to order
  1. Scope Requirements, constraints, volumes, target cost, and the regulatory markets the product has to enter.
  2. Architect Silicon and module selection, block diagram, preliminary BOM, and a costed set of options rather than a single answer.
  3. Design Schematic, layout, and the embedded software needed to prove the board actually works.
  4. Prove Prototype builds, bring-up, validation, and EMC pre-scan before anything is committed to tooling.
  5. Industrialise DFM/DFT, test fixtures, pilot run, and factory transfer with our own people on the floor.
  6. Sustain Supply continuity, component substitutions, revisions, and software maintenance over the product's life.
